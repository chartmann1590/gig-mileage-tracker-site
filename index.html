Private by design

In your language

On first launch you pick your language. The app downloads a free on-device translation pack from Google (ML Kit) for it and translates its screens on your phone; you can change the language any time in Settings. Translations are automatic, so a phrase may read a little differently than a native speaker would put it.

FAQ

Does it track me all the time?

Trip detection runs in the background while tracking is on, with a visible notification. Turn tracking off any time and nothing is recorded.

Where is my location data stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's private database. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Is this tax advice?

No. Reports and estimates are tools for your records; confirm with a tax professional.

Which platforms can I import?

CSV statements with date and amount columns; the importer lets you map the columns.
